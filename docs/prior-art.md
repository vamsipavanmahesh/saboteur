# Prior art

Public papers and documentation consulted for general techniques. No source
code, specs or fixtures from other mutation testing tools are used.

- R. A. DeMillo, R. J. Lipton, F. G. Sayward. "Hints on Test Data Selection:
  Help for the Practicing Programmer." IEEE Computer, 1978. Origin of mutation testing.
- Y. Jia, M. Harman. "An Analysis and Survey of the Development of Mutation
  Testing." IEEE TSE, 2011.
- G. Petrović, M. Ivanković. "State of Mutation Testing at Google." ICSE-SEIP
  2018. Diff-based mutation, arid-node suppression, surfacing survivors in review.
- G. Petrović et al. "Practical Mutation Testing at Scale: A view from Google."
  IEEE TSE, 2021.
- Stryker Mutator, mutation-testing-elements report schema (Apache-2.0):
  https://github.com/stryker-mutator/mutation-testing-elements - output format only.
- Pitest documentation, "descartes"/extreme mutation (method body removal):
  https://pitest.org/ and https://github.com/STAMP-project/pitest-descartes - concept of coarse mutations only.
- Ruby documentation: `Coverage`, `Process`, `Module#class_eval`, Prism.
